Another cookie we tried out of Nicola Lamb's 
[Sift](https://www.nicolalamb.com/sift)
was this sweet-salty treat, inspired by the gigantic cookies from
[Levain in New York](https://levainbakery.com/). Incidentally,
[this Modern MBA video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ha2oNXDZuSg)
about the economics of cookie retail businesses like Crumbl, Levain,
and Mrs. Fields was very interesting.

Anyway, the
[recipe](https://food52.com/recipes/90873-miso-walnut-chocolate-chip-cookies)
starts with making miso walnuts, a candying process using mirin, sugar,
and miso. These get stirred into the thick batter. Each dough ball is
supposed to be 180g, which is insane. I made 90g cookies and they
were still huge.

I liked the saltiness of these cookies but found their texture a bit
dry. But everyone else thought these were worthy of making again.
