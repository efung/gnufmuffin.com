A mango lassi is refreshing with an Indian dinner, its
fruit and dairy taming chili heat. But it's also an excellent
breakfast "smoothie". And now, I've experienced the pleasure
of its flavours in a froyo.

Of course, [kulfi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kulfi) has been
around for hundreds of years. But that's made with cooked milk
and isn't churned. The 
[recipe](https://www.seriouseats.com/mango-lassi-frozen-yogurt-recipe)
I used recently produced a wonderful frozen yogurt that is
a good way to use up yogurt and mangoes.

The ingredient list is pretty straightforward if you've ever had or
made mango lassi. There's added lime zest and juice to up the brightness.
But the real revelation to me was the importance of salt. The
instructions just say to make sure the froyo base has a "lick of
saltiness to it". Surprisingly, that taste was what made every lick
of my cone memorable.
