One of Dr. S' favourite summer desserts is strawberry shortcake. 
This particular version, from one of my favourite bakers and authors,
was a real winner.

The [recipe](https://doriegreenspan.com/recipe/baking-is-all-in-the-hands-buttermilk-biscuit-shortcakes/) 
is by Dorie Greenspan and appeared in her first column for the 
New York Times Magazine. A unique aspect of it is the preparation of
"saucy berries": a pound of berries softened in a syrup made from
herbal tea, citrus zest, and sugar.

Lemon zest features in the biscuits, as well as both buttermilk and
butter. They were flaky, tender and very delicious, warm out of the
oven.

<a data-flickr-embed="true" href="https://www.flickr.com/photos/gnuf/54612361390/in/photostream/" title="Buttermilk Biscuit Shortcakes with Strawberries"><img src="https://live.staticflickr.com/65535/54612361390_6ff56a1834_c.jpg" width="600" height="800" alt="Buttermilk Biscuit Shortcakes with Strawberries"/></a><script async src="//embedr.flickr.com/assets/client-code.js" charset="utf-8"></script>

This was a quick, post-dinner dessert, and I found it pretty easy to 
make. Five of us ate every last crumb, dollop, and seed! Highly
recommended.
