We celebrated the Jewish New Year in Montreal this year, as in the past.
I was tasked with making a honey cake, and decided to try this one
from Deb Perelman.

Known for her popular blog _Smitten Kitchen_, Perelman's 
[recipe](https://smittenkitchen.com/2008/09/majestic-and-moist-honey-cake/recipe)
is chock
full of flavourful ingredients: orange juice, whiskey, coffee, warm
spices. I made this in my sister-in-law's kitchen, and I don't think
there was allspice or cloves so we skipped that.

This turned out nicely, but honestly, I didn't sample very much of it.
I am intrigued that the author says it tastes _phenomenal_ a few days
later. One of the cake rounds was wrapped up and frozen for later, so
no leftovers for me. I did make a mental note that I would repeat
this at some point, as it seems like a delicious, non-traditional
take on a classic Rosh Hashanah cake.
