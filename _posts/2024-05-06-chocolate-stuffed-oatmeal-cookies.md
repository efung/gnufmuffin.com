For a quick snack and thank-you gift, I made these filled cookies one 
evening.  Straight out of the oven, they were gooey and delicious, but 
dried out and got firm later.

The [recipe](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1021516-chocolate-stuffed-oatmeal-cookies) is just an oatmeal cookie batter that you use to sandwich
a ganache filling. I followed one of the reader comments, and instead
of using making a sandwich, I just used one ball of dough and one piece
of ganache to make smaller filled cookies.

These turned out better than I expected. I was anticipating leakage,
but almost all of the cookies held their contents within. The ganache
centre is barely visible so it's a pleasant surprise to discover
the interior. I'm not sure why these cookies didn't taste so good
a few days later, but maybe a few seconds in the microwave would have
refreshed them.
