For a traditional Thanksgiving dinner, one of our guests and my son
requested a pecan pie. I was very happy to oblige, but through a
series of oversights and senior moments, the desserts for this long
weekend did not turn out as planned.

I used recipes from the _Duchess Bake Shop_ cookbook, a celebrated
Edmonton bakery. The pie crust uses a half-half blend of vegetable
shortening and butter and one recipe makes three pie shells worth.
It's very soft and greasy so definitely chill it
thoroughly. After blind baking, my first crust shrunk quite a bit, so make sure to
roll it out thin and as large as possible.

When it came time to making the filling, one thing that stands out
about this recipe is the use of maple syrup, sour cream, and
all-purpose flour. The yield is quite generous and due to
my shrunken crust, I didn't need all of it, but I did save the remainder. 
When it came time to adding the pecans, I couldn't find any, so I substituted some 
Ontario-grown hazelnuts from Grimo Nut Nursery, which we visited on a
trip to Niagara-on-the-Lake earlier this summer.

<a data-flickr-embed="true" href="https://www.flickr.com/photos/gnuf/55607950397/in/dateposted/" title="Walnut and Heartnut Pie"><img src="https://live.staticflickr.com/65535/55607950397_df8f8bc705_c.jpg" width="800" height="600" alt="Walnut and Heartnut Pie"/></a><script async src="//embedr.flickr.com/assets/client-code.js" charset="utf-8"></script>

On Sunday, I made another pie, rolling the crust larger to make
a taller, baked shell, adding the leftover filling from the day
before. In my haste to get this into the oven, I
forgot the nuts! With the top almost
fully set, I dumped a blend of walnuts and heartnuts on the molten 
filling and hoped for the best.

Even both of these maple "pecan" pies contained none of the namesake nuts, 
these were still very delicious pies. The texture is perhaps the most unusual: not firm, not custardy, not pasty
not smooth, but somewhere in the intersection of those textures. Highly recommend.

P.S. The best part is that I found pecans in the fridge downstairs the next day!
