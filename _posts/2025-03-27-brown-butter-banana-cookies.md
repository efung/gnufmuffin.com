Dr. S got me Nicola Lamb's debut cookbook, 
[Sift](https://www.nicolalamb.com/sift) from the library. I had heard
about from someone at work, about how it's very science-y. Flipping
through it, I loved all of the interesting flavour combinations,
using flowers and herbs, and some savoury crossovers. The photos
are also gorgeous and colourful.

The first thing I tried was this banana cookie. The 
[recipe](https://www.latimes.com/recipe/nicola-lamb-no-egg-brown-butter-banana-cookies)
calls for rum-soaked raisins, but I caved and did it with dried 
cranberries instead. The only fussy thing to make is browned butter.

I personally don't think banana belongs in a cookie, so my highest 
praise for these is that they are nice and soft. But everyone else, 
include kids, seemed to like them.
