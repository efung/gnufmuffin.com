While coffee cake can be pretty plain, I had another surplus of Greek
yogurt to save from the compost. This turned out to be a pleasant 
surprise, with a moist flavourful cake, topped with a nutty, fragrant
streusel.

That delicious topping is made with whole wheat flour, and both cinnamon
and cardamom. It will seem like you have too much, but trust me, you
definitely want all of it.

I trust almost every 
[recipe](https://www.seriouseats.com/easy-coffee-cake-recipe)
from Stella Parks because she's so thorough and detailed in her
write-up. This one was no exception.

Because a 9x13" cake is a lot of cake, I cut the leftovers into squares
and froze them. I can now zap a piece for an afternoon snack anytime
I need a little treat.
