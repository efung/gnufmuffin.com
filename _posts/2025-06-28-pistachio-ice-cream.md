Recently, I noticed that Costco sells products from 
[Early Robin](https://premiernuts.ca/collections/bottle-collection),
a brand from a Canadian company called Premier Nuts. What
caught my eye was the fact that their pistachio cream (a blend of
nuts, milk powder, sugar) contained **70%** nuts. Every other
pistachio spread I've tried (several Italian brands 
[purchased at Eataly](https://shoptoronto.eataly.ca/products/pistachio-spread-1),
a Turkish one purchased from [Mado](https://madocanada.com/), 
even the 
[one at Soma](https://www.somachocolate.com/products/majoun-pistachio?variant=29059165837)),
had far less pistachio. What was even more exciting was that Early Robin
also sold a 99% pistachio paste! I quickly added a 
[two-pack](https://www.costco.ca/early-robin-pistachio-spreads-2--250-g.product.4000360485.html) 
of one of each to my Costco cart. At $40 CAD for 500g, this works out to
a cost of $8 / 100g, a definite splurge, but cheaper than any other
comparable product.

The [recipe](https://www.reddit.com/r/icecreamery/comments/1llkvkd/comment/n00z1vr/)
comes from the _Van Leeuwen Artisan Ice Cream_ book. It's a regular
custard ice cream base, made with a bit more cream than milk, and
six egg yolks. It calls for a quarter cup of pistachio paste. We already
polished off the pistachio cream, so I used the one that was almost 
pure nuts.

What a revelation that I could make such a delicious ice cream at home!
The pistachio flavour is pronounced, and the sweetness and richness
is just right. The colour is a wonderful, pale green. I think I could even
increase the amount of pistachio paste some more. Or experiment with
mixing in toasted pistachios or brittle!
