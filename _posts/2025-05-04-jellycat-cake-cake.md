For my daughter's birthday, she pulled up a few short videos of what
looked like a 
[Jellycat cake](https://us.jellycat.com/amuseables-birthday-cake/), 
made out of cake!

Of course, that's exactly what it was. To achieve this vision, I used
the Chinese Sponge Cake recipe from _Mooncakes and Milk Bread_ by
Kristina Cho. It's essentially a chiffon cake, made with whipped eggs
and oil.

The cake layers turned out perfectly, very light, evenly browned.
We then made stabilized whipped cream (also in the book), which is
Chantilly (sweetened) cream with some gelatin. To one batch,
my daughter added enough cocoa powder to tint it light brown.

Then, the fun began! She piped teardrops of plain cream along the 
perimeter of the bottom layer, then added some sliced strawberries
to the center. After piping blobs of cream on the top, she used
a small disposable piping bag to build up the legs, and then piped
melted chocolate to make the face.

<a data-flickr-embed="true" href="https://www.flickr.com/photos/gnuf/54599820496/in/photostream/" title="Jellycat Cake Cake"><img src="https://live.staticflickr.com/65535/54599820496_0667ed0141_c.jpg" width="800" height="600" alt="Jellycat Cake Cake"/></a><script async src="//embedr.flickr.com/assets/client-code.js" charset="utf-8"></script>

I grew up eating cakes like this, so it was very nostalgic for me. She
did note it was a bit eggy tasting, but it was the appearance that
drew the gasps and praise from her friends.
