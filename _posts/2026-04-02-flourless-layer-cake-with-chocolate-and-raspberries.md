For this year's Passover meals, we were in Montreal, and I made
two different desserts ahead of time while in Toronto. One was an
old favourite based on a Claudia Roden recipe involving boiling a
whole orange. But this time, I used a mix of navel orange, clementine,
and frozen yuzu peel. For night two, I tried something new.

The [recipe](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/774296693-gluten-free-layer-cake-with-chocolate-and-raspberries)
for this layer cake is from the New York Times. It doesn't have flour
or dairy, so satisfies many diets and this particular holiday.

The baked and cooled cake layers were very delicate and hard to remove
from the parchment, as warned in the directions. I had to cut them to
fit the large box that I purchased.

<a data-flickr-embed="true" href="https://www.flickr.com/photos/gnuf/55271875992/in/photostream/" title="Flourless Layer Cake With Chocolate and Raspberries"><img src="https://live.staticflickr.com/65535/55271875992_a364445488_c.jpg" width="800" height="600" alt="Flourless Layer Cake With Chocolate and Raspberries"/></a><script async src="//embedr.flickr.com/assets/client-code.js" charset="utf-8"></script>

Making the filling was interesting: after melting chocolate in a sugar
syrup, you whisk the saucepan over ice until it thickens somewhat.
Mine didn't firm to a pudding consistency and was still pourable. But
I carefully spread it to the edges of the layers. 

<a data-flickr-embed="true" href="https://www.flickr.com/photos/gnuf/55272794891/in/dateposted/" title="Flourless Layer Cake With Chocolate and Raspberries"><img src="https://live.staticflickr.com/65535/55272794891_e5a5cd9344_c.jpg" width="800" height="600" alt="Flourless Layer Cake With Chocolate and Raspberries"/></a><script async src="//embedr.flickr.com/assets/client-code.js" charset="utf-8"></script>

My favourite part were the sweet-tart raspberries. I tore them up and 
tried to press them flat inside the cake. I had my daughter place
the remaining berries on the top.

<a data-flickr-embed="true" href="https://www.flickr.com/photos/gnuf/55271876042/in/photostream/" title="Flourless Layer Cake With Chocolate and Raspberries"><img src="https://live.staticflickr.com/65535/55271876042_f80acf3406_c.jpg" width="800" height="600" alt="Flourless Layer Cake With Chocolate and Raspberries"/></a><script async src="//embedr.flickr.com/assets/client-code.js" charset="utf-8"></script>

Quite a bit more work than an orange-almond cake, but I think it was 
worth it. It's very chocolatey, but not cloying, due to the bright
raspberries.
