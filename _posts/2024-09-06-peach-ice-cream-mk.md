A colleague shared an ice cream recipe that she had been tweaking,
and I decided to try it, with some late-season peaches.

Her version is based on the [recipe](https://cookingbythebook.com/dessert-digest/lebovitzs-peach-ice-cream/) 
by David Lebovitz. The differences are that she omits the water, 
uses only 1/2 cup heavy cream, replaces the sour cream with 1 cup yogurt,
and replaces vanilla with peach brandy.

This ice cream was pretty good, nice a tangy, with a good peach flavour.
But I didn't like the Archer's Peach Schnapps I used, too artificial.
I'd make this again, but with a more natural peach brandy.
